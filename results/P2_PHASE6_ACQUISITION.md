# P2 Phase 6 — Full MOSDAC Data Acquisition Report

**Date**: September 1, 2026  
**Source**: ISRO / IMD MOSDAC Open Search (`https://mosdac.gov.in`)  
**Product**: `3DIMG_L1C_SGP` / `3RIMG_L1C_SGP` (TIR-1 $10.8\ \mu\text{m}$ Channel)

---

## 1. Full Historical Acquisition Scope

The full archival acquisition spans **25 discrete cyclone systems** across the Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea from 2014 to 2024:
* **Super Cyclonic Storms (SuCS)**: *Amphan (2020), Kyarr (2019)*
* **Extremely Severe Cyclonic Storms (ESCS)**: *Fani (2019), Mocha (2023), Tauktae (2021), Hudhud (2014), Biparjoy (2023)*
* **Very Severe Cyclonic Storms (VSCS)**: *Titli (2018), Bulbul (2019), Gaja (2018), Yaas (2021)*
* **Severe Cyclonic Storms (SCS)**: *Michaung (2023), Remal (2024), Asani (2022), Dana (2024)*
* **Cyclonic Storms (CS)**: *Fengal (2024), Gulab (2021), Sitrang (2022), Jawad (2021), Midhili (2023)*
* **Deep Depressions (DD)**: *BOB 01 (2018), BOB 02 (2019), BOB 05 (2021)*
* **Depressions (D)**: *BOB 01 (2019), BOB 02 (2020)*
